facts of factor:

    Factors = possible choices
    
    common factors =  shared choices
    
    Highest common factors = best option from the shared choices

        hcf = gcf = gcd # all are same

        HCF (1, x) = 1

        HCF (x, y) = x, if y is a multiple of x. (HCF (3, 6) = 3

        if the HCF of two numbers is 1, the numbers said coprimes or relatively prime. the numbers can be primes, or composites, or both.


    Every number has two factors that is 1 and the number itself.
    Multiples of a number are endless.

    Every factor of a given number is less than or equal to that number.
    Every multiple of a given number is greater than or equal to that number.
